# Ads Manager playbook (version 1, 20 Nov 2026)

These are **starting rules from common practice**, not promises. The owner's own targets always win. This file is
meant to be replaced, section by section, by the playbook of an owner who runs ads well (Abu's guide is planned). Keep
the section names, so the skill keeps working.

## Reading the numbers
- **The result** is what the owner said counts (a purchase, a lead, a message, a booking). Judge everything by
  **cost per result** against the owner's **target**. Clicks and likes only explain why.
- Always compare **the last 7 days with the 7 before**, and look at the month so far. Name the date range and Meta's
  attribution setting (usually 7 days after a click, 1 day after a view).
- **Too little data is an answer:** an ad set that has spent less than about **2× the target cost per result**, or ran
  less than **7 days**, is "too early to say". Don't stop or scale it yet, unless it is plainly wasting money (below).
- **Meta's learning phase:** an ad set needs roughly **50 results in 7 days** to settle. Big edits (a budget change of
  more than about 20 %, new targeting, a new creative) restart learning. Fewer, larger ad sets learn faster than many
  small ones.

## Stop
- Spent **2× the target cost per result** or more with **no result**.
- Cost per result **above 1.5× the target for 7 days**, with enough spend to judge.
- **Tired ad:** frequency above about **3** (the same people see it again and again) and the click rate **30 % below**
  its first week. Then propose a new ad in its place, not just a pause.

## Scale
- Cost per result **at or below target for 7 days** and stable: raise the budget by **at most 20 %**, and **not more
  often than every 3 to 4 days**. Always inside the monthly limit.
- **Never scale something that is still learning.** Let it finish first.

## New ads
- **At most 3 new ads a week**, in the ad set that works best. Change **one thing** at a time: the opening line, the
  image or the offer, so you know what made the difference.
- **Build on the winner:** the same promise said differently, the same picture with a new opening, a customer
  question as the hook.
- **The first line carries the ad.** Say plainly who it is for and what they get. Use local and concrete words
  ("Kassel", "this week", a real price from the owner's data).

## When clicks are good but results aren't
The ad works and the page after it doesn't. Tell the owner what you see: the page loads slowly, the offer differs
from the ad, or the form is long. Don't touch the website yourself.

## Tracking
- If results are missing that should be there (purchases, leads), check the tracking quality first (the dataset and
  pixel read tools). If it looks broken, tell the owner what to check **before** you judge any ad.

## Money
- **Forecast** = spent this month + (sum of the daily budgets still running × days left this month). It must stay
  under the owner's monthly limit, always.
- **Lifetime budgets** count in full when they end this month.
